# MergeTasks Marketing Site — Final Design Direction

## Revised Approach: "White Editorial — The Stripe/Linear Playbook"

**Design Movement:** Clean editorial minimalism. Stripe, Linear, Vercel marketing DNA. Typography and whitespace do the heavy lifting. No dark heroes, no purple gradients, no AI-slop.

**Core Principles:**
1. White canvas, black type — the confidence comes from what you DON'T add
2. Product screenshots as the visual centerpiece — real prototype UI in browser mockups
3. Typography hierarchy creates all the drama — size contrast between 72px headlines and 16px body
4. Purple (#654BF9) is surgical — buttons and text links ONLY, never backgrounds or decorative

**Color Palette:**
- Background: #FFFFFF (primary), #F9FAFB (alternating sections)
- Text: #111827 (headlines), #374151 (body), #6B7280 (secondary/captions)
- Accent: #654BF9 (buttons and links only)
- Borders: #E5E7EB (subtle dividers)

**Layout:** Asymmetric where possible. Left-aligned headlines. Product mockups float right or break out of the content column. Max-width 1200px content. Generous vertical padding (120px+ between sections).

**Typography:** Albert Sans throughout. 700/800 for headlines, 400 for body, 500 for nav/buttons. JetBrains Mono for stats only.

**What we're NOT doing:**
- No dark/black hero sections
- No purple gradients or glows
- No abstract 3D renders
- No centered-everything layouts
- No rounded card grids
- No generic stock photography
